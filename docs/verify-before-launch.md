# Verify before launch

Every claim the product makes about insurance rules or its own legal
positioning that needs checking against a real policy document, regulation,
or legal opinion before this goes near a real user.

## Open

- **"Audit tool, not intermediary" positioning (section 10).** The
  distinction is legal, not cosmetic, per the spec's own framing. Drafting
  grievance letters that cite specific policy clauses and regulatory
  provisions sits close to the line between "reading documents for the
  user" and "acting on the policyholder's behalf in the claim." Needs
  explicit legal sign-off before section 5.4 ships, not just interface and
  copy discipline.
- **Proportionate deduction rule variance post the IRDAI Master Circular on
  Health Insurance Business (29 May 2024).** Section 4 notes several
  insurers changed or removed the clause. Each seeded rule set (stage 3
  golden fixtures onward) needs its source policy wording checked, not
  assumed from the general pattern.
